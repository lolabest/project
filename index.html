How to Play

  • Aim with the mouse, touch, or arrow keys.
  • Shoot to snap a bubble onto the hexagonal board.
  • Match 3 or more of the same color to pop them.
  • Disconnected clusters fall for bonus points.
  • Clear the board to win. Crossing the red line loses.

Works offline — just open index.html.
